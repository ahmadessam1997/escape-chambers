Privacy Policy

Escape: 20 Chambers  ·  com.ahmadessam.escapechambers
Effective 13 August 2026
The short version. The game itself is offline — your chamber progress and your hint wallet never leave your phone. But the game is free and pays for itself with ads, and the advertising and purchase services it uses do collect data. That is set out in full below, and it matches the Data safety answers on the Google Play listing exactly.

What stays on your device

  • Which chambers you have escaped and which are unlocked.
  • Your hint wallet and whether you own the unlimited-hints unlock.
  • Nothing here is transmitted anywhere. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

There is no account, no login, and no cloud save.

What the third-party services collect

Google AdMob — advertising

Ads appear between chambers, and you can choose to watch a video to earn hints. To serve and measure those ads, Google AdMob collects:

DataWhy
Advertising IDAd delivery, frequency capping, fraud prevention
Approximate location (derived from IP address)Regionally appropriate ads and legal compliance
Device and other identifiersAd delivery, analytics, fraud prevention
App interactionsAd performance measurement
Crash logs and diagnosticsReliability and abuse prevention

Ad requests from this game are capped to the “General” content rating, and the game does not request behaviourally-targeted advertising for children. See how Google uses data from apps that use its services.

RevenueCat — purchases

If you buy anything, RevenueCat records the purchase so it can be restored on a new device. It receives your purchase history and an anonymous app user ID that it generates itself. It does not receive your name or email. See the RevenueCat privacy policy.

Google Play — billing

Payment is handled entirely by Google Play. The game never sees or stores a card number, a billing address, or any payment detail. Google Play draws its own purchase sheet and hands the app back only a success or failure result.

What is never collected

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or contacts.
  • Precise location, camera, microphone, photos, or files.
  • Anything you type — the game has no text input at all.

Children

The game is rated for everyone, but because it uses the advertising ID it is not directed at children under 13 and is not part of Google Play’s Designed for Families programme. If you are under 13, please ask a parent or guardian before playing.

Your choices

  • Reset or delete your advertising ID on the device: Settings → Google → All services → Ads. Deleting it stops personalised advertising.
  • Remove ads with the one-time in-app purchase. Ads between chambers stop. Hint videos remain available if you choose to watch them — they are opt-in and are the free way to earn hints.
  • Delete everything — see how to delete your data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address before the update that relies on it ships.
