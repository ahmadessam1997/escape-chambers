Privacy Policy

Escape: 20 Chambers  ·  com.ahmadessam.escapechambers
Effective 2 October 2026 (version 1.6.0). First published 13 August 2026.
The short version. The game itself is offline — your chamber progress and your hint wallet never leave your phone. But the game is free and pays for itself with ads, and the advertising, analytics and purchase services it uses do collect data. That is set out in full below, and it matches the Data safety answers on the Google Play listing exactly.

What stays on your device

  • Which chambers you have escaped and which are unlocked.
  • Your hint wallet and whether you own the unlimited-hints unlock.
  • Your best run in each chamber, the day you last claimed the free daily hint, and your daily-chamber streak.
  • Whether you switched the daily reminder on or off.
  • This save file is not transmitted anywhere. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

There is no account, no login, and no cloud save.

What the third-party services collect

Google AdMob — advertising

Ads appear as a banner on the rooms menu, between chambers, as a small card on the escape screen, and sometimes when you return to the game after time away. You can also choose to watch a video to earn hints or to skip a chamber. To serve and measure those ads, Google AdMob collects:

DataWhy
Advertising IDAd delivery, frequency capping, fraud prevention
Approximate location (derived from IP address)Regionally appropriate ads and legal compliance
Device and other identifiersAd delivery, analytics, fraud prevention
App interactionsAd performance measurement
Crash logs and diagnosticsReliability and abuse prevention

Ad requests from this game are capped to the “General” content rating, and the game does not request behaviourally-targeted advertising for children. See how Google uses data from apps that use its services.

Google Analytics for Firebase — how the game is played

The game sends Google Analytics for Firebase a small set of gameplay events so the developer can see where players get stuck and what is used: a chamber being started or escaped (with its number, time taken, stars and hints used), a hint being used, the hint shop being opened, a purchase being started or completed (the product only — never payment details), an ad being shown and its format, a rewarded video being finished, a choice on the “rate this game” prompt, the daily hint and daily chamber, the “out of hints” offer being shown, a chamber being skipped, and a tap on a link to another of our games. Firebase attaches an app-instance identifier, the advertising ID, and general device information (model, OS version, language, country) to those events. Nothing you could type is included, because the game has no text input. Google keeps these events for a limited period (2 to 14 months depending on our Google Analytics setting) and then deletes them automatically. See Privacy and Security in Firebase.

Notifications — the daily reminder

If you allow it, the game schedules one reminder (“Your free hint is ready”) for about a day after you last played. It is a local notification: it is scheduled on your own device, no notification token is created, and nothing is sent to any server to deliver it. The game only asks for the notification permission the first time you claim a daily hint, never when it starts. You can switch the reminder off at any time under Hints & extras, or in Android’s notification settings.

RevenueCat — purchases

If you buy anything, RevenueCat records the purchase so it can be restored on a new device. It receives your purchase history and an anonymous app user ID that it generates itself. It does not receive your name or email. See the RevenueCat privacy policy.

Google Play — billing

Payment is handled entirely by Google Play. The game never sees or stores a card number, a billing address, or any payment detail. Google Play draws its own purchase sheet and hands the app back only a success or failure result.

What is never collected

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or contacts.
  • Precise location, camera, microphone, photos, or files.
  • Anything you type — the game has no text input at all.

Children

The game is rated for everyone, but because it uses the advertising ID it is not directed at children under 13 and is not part of Google Play’s Designed for Families programme. If you are under 13, please ask a parent or guardian before playing.

Your choices

  • Reset or delete your advertising ID on the device: Settings → Google → All services → Ads. Deleting it stops personalised advertising.
  • Remove ads with the one-time in-app purchase. Every ad format stops, including the optional hint videos. Free hints still come from escaping chambers, the daily hint and the daily chamber.
  • Change your ad-consent choice where the law gives you one (EEA, UK and some US states): Hints & extras → Privacy choices.
  • Turn the daily reminder off: Hints & extras → Daily reminder.
  • Analytics data is tied to the advertising ID and an app-instance ID, not to your name. Deleting the advertising ID and clearing the app’s data (or uninstalling) cuts the link to what was collected before; deletion requests are explained on the delete your data page.
  • Delete everything — see how to delete your data.
