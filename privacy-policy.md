# Privacy Policy — Escape: 20 Chambers

**App:** Escape: 20 Chambers (`com.ahmadessam.escapechambers`)
**Effective date:** 2026-07-11

## The short version
Escape: 20 Chambers collects **no data**. None.

## Details
- The game runs entirely offline. It makes no network requests and requires no internet permission.
- No personal information is collected, stored, transmitted, sold, or shared.
- No accounts, no analytics, no advertising SDKs, no tracking of any kind.
- Game progress (levels completed / high scores) is stored **only on your device** using the app's local storage. Uninstalling the app deletes it.
- The game does not access contacts, location, camera, microphone, files, or any other device data.

## Children
The game is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.
