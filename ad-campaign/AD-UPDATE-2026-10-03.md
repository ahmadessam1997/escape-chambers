# Escape Chambers — ad update for v1.7.0 (3 Oct 2026)

What changed in the app: 1.7.0 renames the game from "Escape: 20 Chambers" to
**Escape Chambers**, with no number in the name (the owner's decision). It now has **30 chambers** plus a
**Daily Chamber**: one fresh room a day, a streak, and a free hint every day. You can also skip a chamber. Remove
Ads removes every ad, including the rewarded hint videos. None of the old ad text
mentioned ads, so only the "20" claims and the name had to change.
